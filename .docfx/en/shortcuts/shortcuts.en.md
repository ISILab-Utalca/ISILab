# Shortcuts List

LBS has shortcuts to quickly access some of its different tools. This section presents a list of the shortcuts offered by Level Builder Sidekick.

## List of LBS shortcuts

> [!NOTE]
> Shortcuts are context-dependent. The context in which each shortcut works is defined by its category.
